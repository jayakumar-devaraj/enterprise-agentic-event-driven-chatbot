Acme Employee Handbook

Vacation. Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year. Unused days up to 5 carry over to the next year.

Parental leave. Every parent receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave.

Expenses. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Purchases over 500 dollars need written approval from a manager.

Remote work. The yearly home-office stipend is 600 dollars. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
